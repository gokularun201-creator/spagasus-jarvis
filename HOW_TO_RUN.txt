=====================================================
SPAGASUS JARVIS - STANDALONE PORTABLE EDITION
=====================================================

HOW TO RUN ON ANY COMPUTER:
1. Double-click "SPAGASUS JARVIS.exe"
   - No Python installation is needed!
   - All models, dependencies, and engines are included.
   - The app will automatically launch in a clean standalone window.

OPTIONAL:
- Double-click "Create_Desktop_Shortcut.vbs" to put a shortcut
  directly on your Desktop.
- You can also run "start_spagasus.bat" to view the console.

MOBILE COMPANION:
- Pair your Android phone at http://<this-pc-ip>:8790/m using the
  PHONE_TOKEN in .env (default: spagasus-local-dev-token).
